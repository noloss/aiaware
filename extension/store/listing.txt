# ── DETAILED DESCRIPTION ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AI Aware scans the text you're about to send to AI chat services (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT) and warns you when it detects sensitive content — AWS keys, passwords, personal data, or prompt-injection patterns.

🔴 Security Intercept
A blocking modal appears for HIGH-risk messages, giving you a chance to review before anything is submitted to the AI.

🛡️ Zero-Network — Your Data Stays on Your Device
Every analysis runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. No text, metadata, or usage data is ever sent to our servers — because we have no servers. Settings are stored in chrome.storage.local on your machine only.

Shadow Block
Automatically blocks the Enter key when a HIGH-risk alert is active, so you can't accidentally send a flagged message.

Supports: Claude (claude.ai) · Gemini (gemini.google.com) · ChatGPT (chat.openai.com)

Privacy policy: https://otto-olsson.github.io/promptsentinel/privacy_policy.html
