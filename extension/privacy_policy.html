Privacy Policy

Prompt Masker — Last updated: 2026-05-14

1. What the Extension Does

Prompt Masker is a Chrome extension that monitors text you type into AI chat services (Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini) and warns you in real time when potentially sensitive data — such as API keys, credit card numbers, or personal identifiers — is detected before it is sent.

All detection happens locally in your browser. No text you type is ever transmitted to any server operated by the extension developer.

2. Data Collected

Prompt Masker collects no personal data and sends no data over the network. Specifically:

  • Your typed text is processed entirely on-device and never leaves your browser.
  • An optional audit log (last 50 triggered warnings) is stored in chrome.storage.local on your device only.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage data is collected.
  • No cookies are set. No remote servers are contacted.

3. No Accuracy Guarantee

The extension uses pattern-matching heuristics to detect sensitive data. This means:

  • It may miss sensitive data (false negatives) — some sensitive content may not be detected.
  • It may produce false alarms (false positives) — some harmless text may be flagged as sensitive.

You remain responsible for reviewing what you submit to AI services. The extension is a best-effort safety net, not a guarantee.

4. Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab / scripting — to inject the DLP content script into AI chat pages.
  • storage — to persist your settings and audit log in chrome.storage.local.

No host permissions beyond the supported AI chat sites are requested.

5. Children's Privacy

Prompt Masker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the extension in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us using the address below.
