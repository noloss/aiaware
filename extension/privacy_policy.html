Prompt Masker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-10
🔒
Zero-Network Extension

Prompt Masker operates entirely on your device. No user conversation text, no typed input, and no personal information is ever transmitted to any external server — by us or any third party. We have no servers.

1. What Prompt Masker Does

Prompt Masker is a Chrome browser extension that inspects text you are about to submit to AI chat interfaces (such as Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT) and warns you when it detects sensitive or risky content — for example, API keys, passwords, personal identifiers, or prompt-injection patterns. All detection logic runs locally in your browser using JavaScript; no text is ever sent elsewhere.

2. Data We Collect

We collect nothing. Prompt Masker does not collect, process on a remote server, transmit, or share any data about you or your activity.

Data type Accessed locally? Sent to a server? Stored persistently?
Text you type in AI chat Yes — to run the DLP check in your browser NEVER NEVER
Extension preferences (enabled/disabled) Yes NEVER Yes — chrome.storage.local on your device only
Analytics / telemetry / crash reports NOT COLLECTED
Browsing history NOT COLLECTED
Personal identifiers (name, email, IP) NOT COLLECTED
No user conversation data is transmitted to external servers — not to our servers, not to third-party servers, not to anyone. Your typed text never leaves your browser.

3. Permissions We Request and Why

  • storage — saves your on/off preference to chrome.storage.local. This data lives on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Content scripts on claude.ai, gemini.google.com, chat.openai.com — injects the DLP scanner UI into these pages so it can read the text field before you press Send. All analysis runs in your browser; no data from these pages is sent elsewhere.

4. Third-Party Services

Prompt Masker does not integrate with, call, or depend on any third-party service, API, analytics platform, or remote resource. The extension bundles no external scripts, fonts, or tracking pixels.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer any user data to outside parties — because we never receive any user data in the first place.

6. Children's Privacy

Prompt Masker does not knowingly collect information from any users, including children under the age of 13. No personal data of any kind is collected by this extension.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We will not make changes that weaken these privacy guarantees without clearly communicating them in the extension's release notes.
