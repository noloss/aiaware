AI Aware — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-10
🔒
Zero-Network Extension

AI Aware operates entirely on your device. No user conversation data, no typed text, and no personal information is ever transmitted to any external server — by us or any third party.

1. What AI Aware Does

AI Aware is a browser extension that monitors text you are about to submit to AI chat interfaces (such as Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT) and warns you if it detects patterns that may indicate sensitive or risky content (e.g., credentials, personal data, prompt-injection attempts).

2. Data We Collect

We collect nothing. AI Aware does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any data about you or your activity.

All processing happens locally on your machine:

  • Text analysis runs inside your browser using JavaScript — never sent to a server.
  • Your extension settings (e.g., enabled/disabled state) are saved to chrome.storage.local — your device only.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage statistics are collected.
No user conversation data is transmitted to external servers — not to our servers, not to third-party servers, not to anyone. Your typed text never leaves your device.

3. Permissions We Request

  • storage — stores your preferences locally in chrome.storage.local. Data never leaves your device.
  • Content scripts on claude.ai, gemini.google.com, chat.openai.com — injects the DLP monitor UI into AI chat pages. No data from these pages is ever sent anywhere.

4. Third-Party Services

AI Aware does not integrate with, call, or depend on any third-party service, API, or remote resource. The extension contains no external scripts, fonts, or trackers.

5. Children's Privacy

AI Aware does not knowingly collect information from any users, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. We will not make changes that weaken these privacy guarantees without clearly communicating them.
